# Smart Pantry — Privacy-First Expiration Tracker
Smart Pantry is a lightweight, local-first Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help you organize your food inventory, keep track of expiration dates, and minimize household food waste without sacrificing your privacy.

## Try it live: 
https://smart-pantry-ruby.vercel.app/

## 🔒 Privacy-First Architecture
100% Local-First Storage: Your pantry data never leaves your device. Everything is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. No centralized servers, no background tracking, no registration required.

End-to-End Encrypted Sharing: If you choose to share or sync your product list with family members or housemates, the exported data is fully encrypted before leaving your device. Only people with your shared link/key can decrypt and access your list.


## No Ads, No Subscriptions: Built as an open, distraction-free tool designed purely for utility.


# ✨ Key Features
Visual Status Overview: Instant color-coded dashboard (Red / Yellow / Green) to identify items that are expired, expiring soon (within 3 days), or safe.


## Smart Quick-Actions:

Consume & Reorder: Mark items as consumed to automatically add them to your Shopping List.

Freeze & Extend: Move items to the freezer with a single tap to automatically extend their shelf life by +30 days.

Location & Category Organization: Easily filter and manage items across different storage zones (Refrigerator, Pantry, Freezer) and categories.

Integrated Barcode & Smart Input: Fast item entry using your device's camera via Open Food Facts integration, or quick text parsing.

PWA & Offline Support: Installable on iOS, Android, and Desktop directly from the browser. Works seamlessly without an active internet connection.

Automated Shopping List: Generate and manage a clean shopping list based on consumed items.
